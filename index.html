Hi, I'm Melegy.

I'm Abdelqader Elmelegy, or Melegy: an ACE Certified Personal Trainer and Head Coach. I've been training for 18 years.

That's long enough to have tried a lot of things, gotten some very right, gotten some very wrong, dealt with injuries, hit plateaus and changed how I train more than once. It's also long enough to learn that good training is usually about more than adding weight and hoping for the best.

Over the years I've trained across strength and conditioning, physique development, calisthenics, mobility, corrective exercise and athletic performance. I've also spent a lot of time outside the traditional gym through skydiving, parkour and stunt performance. All of it shaped how I coach.

I don't believe there's one perfect training program. There's a program that makes sense for you: your history, your life, and how your body responds to training.

I'm not here to hand you a plan, wish you luck and disappear. I want to know what's working, what isn't, what you're struggling with, and what needs to change.

More volume isn't automatically better, and soreness isn't proof a workout was good. If something hurts, the answer isn't always "never do that exercise again." Context matters.

Sometimes the answer is more training.
Sometimes it's less.
Sometimes you need to push harder.
Sometimes you need to stop doing the thing that keeps pissing off the same injury.
And sometimes the problem isn't your motivation — your program just doesn't make much sense.

That's the kind of coaching I believe in:
Understand the person.
Understand the goal.
Build the plan.
Pay attention to what happens.
Adjust when necessary.

I don't promise magic. I promise I'll pay attention.
