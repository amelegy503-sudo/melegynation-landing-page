Hi, I'm Melegy.

I'm Abdelqader Elmelegy, an ACE Certified Personal Trainer and Head Coach. I've been training for 18 years.

Over those years, I've gone through different training methods, sports, disciplines, injuries, plateaus, and plenty of trial and error. More importantly, I've kept learning. That experience became the foundation of how I coach today.

My approach sits around strength, physique development, movement quality, corrective exercise, mobility, conditioning, and performance. I don't believe everyone needs the same training plan. I look at the person in front of me: their goal, experience, schedule, limitations, and how they respond to training. Then I build from there.

Outside coaching, I'm involved in skydiving, stunt performance, and parkour. Those disciplines have made strength, movement quality, control, and physical capability an important part of how I train and coach.
