Hi, I'm Melegy.

I'm Abdelqader Elmelegy, an ACE Certified Personal Trainer and Head Coach.

I've been training for 18 years. Over those years, I've gone through different training methods, sports, disciplines, injuries, plateaus, and plenty of trial and error.

More importantly, I've kept learning.

That experience became the foundation of how I coach today.

My approach sits around strength, physique development, movement quality, corrective exercise, mobility, conditioning and performance. I don't believe everyone needs the same training plan. I look at the person in front of me — their goal, experience, schedule, limitations and how they respond to training — and build from there.

Outside coaching I'm involved in skydiving, stunt performance and parkour. Those disciplines have made strength, movement quality, control and physical capability an important part of how I train and coach.

I'm a coach, not a doctor or physiotherapist, and I don't diagnose or treat injuries.

How much does online coaching cost?

$130 per month. That covers personalized training, nutrition guidance, weekly check-ins, WhatsApp support, progress tracking and program adjustments.

Who is online coaching for?

People who train — or want to train seriously — and need better structure, progression and accountability.

Do I need a gym?

No. Programs can be built around a full gym, home setup, minimal equipment or calisthenics depending on your goal.

Is nutrition included?

Yes. Nutrition guidance is part of the coaching and is adjusted as your progress changes.

Can you work around injuries or limitations?

Within the scope of coaching, yes. I provide injury-aware programming, corrective exercise and exercise-based support for returning to training. I don't diagnose or treat injuries; where appropriate, training should be coordinated with the relevant healthcare professional.
