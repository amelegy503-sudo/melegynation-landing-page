MELEGY: IMAGE GUIDE
===================
Drop your photos in this folder with EXACTLY these filenames (.jpg).
The files currently in this folder are labelled PLACEHOLDERS (800px wide). Overwrite each one with your photo.
Export no wider than 2000px, JPEG quality ~80, and compress (squoosh.app).
Aim for under 300 KB each (hero under 250 KB). Update the alt text in index.html to match each photo.

PHOTO 1  hero.jpg            4:5 or taller (~1600x2000). You coaching or training; real gym, natural light.
PHOTO 2  about.jpg           4:5. Candid, in your training space. Not a mirror selfie.
PHOTO 3  athletic.jpg        4:5. You in a calisthenics skill (handstand, pull-up, dip) or strong athletic movement.
PHOTO 4  client-01-before.jpg, client-01-after.jpg, client-02-before.jpg, client-02-after.jpg
                             4:5 each. Same pose, light and distance in both. Optional per case study.

                             9:16 phone screenshots of real client messages (with permission).
PHOTO 6  lifestyle.jpg       4:5. Behind the scenes: prepping a session, a candid coaching moment.
PHOTO 7  coaching.jpg        4:5. A check-in, a form correction, or a WhatsApp chat (with permission).
